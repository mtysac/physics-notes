Kinematics

Scalars vs Vectors

ScalarsVectors
Magnitude onlyMagnitude AND Direction

Quantities

QuantitySI UnitDefinition
Distance (scalar) m Length travelled
Displacement (vector) m Length travelled with respect to direction
Speed (scalar) \(\text{m/s}\) or \(\text{m s}^{-1}\) Rate of change of distance
Velocity (vector) \(\text{m/s}\) or \(\text{m s}^{-1}\) Rate of change of displacement
Acceleration (vector) \(\text{m/s}^2\) or \(\text{m s}^{-2}\) Rate of change of velocity

Formulas

\(\displaystyle v = \frac{\Delta x}{t}\)
\(\displaystyle s = \frac{d}{t}\)
\(\Delta x = V_1 t + at^2\)
\(V_2 = V_1 + at\)
\(\displaystyle a = \frac{V_F - V_I}{t}\)
\(\displaystyle \Delta x = \frac{1}{2}(V_1 + V_2)t\)
\(V_2^2 = V_1^2 + 2a\Delta x\)
\(V_{12} = V_{1E} - V_{2E}\)
\(V_{AC} = V_{AB} + V_{BC}\)

Variable Key

\(v\) = velocity
\(s\) = speed
\(d\) = distance
\(\Delta x\) = displacement (some may use \(d\))
\(t\) = time
\(a\) = acceleration
\(V_F = V_2\) = Final Velocity
\(V_I = V_1\) = Initial Velocity
\(a = g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\)
\(1\ \text{m/s} = 3.6\ \text{km/h}\)
